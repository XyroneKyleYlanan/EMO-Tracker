Risk Assessment and Analysis — EMD Tracker

How to use: Select the table below, copy (Ctrl+C), and paste into Word or Google Docs. It pastes as a native table. Risk Level is derived from a standard Likelihood × Impact matrix.

Risk ID Risk Description Category Likelihood Impact Risk Level Mitigation Strategy
R1 Server laptop failure or downtime, making the system inaccessible to all users Technical Medium High High Keep the server laptop plugged in and fully charged during operation (the battery serves as a built-in UPS against power interruptions); designate a backup device with the environment pre-installed; maintain documented setup instructions for quick restoration.
R2 Data loss due to the absence of automated cloud backup (LAN-only deployment) Data Medium High High Perform regular local database backups using mysqldump; store backup copies on a separate external drive; schedule backups before and after major events.
R3 Network (WiFi or hotspot) unavailability preventing devices from reaching the server Technical Medium High High Use a dedicated router or mobile hotspot for the deployment; prepare a backup network connection; the system requires only a local network and no internet access.
R4 Single point of failure — the database, backend, and frontend all run on one device Technical Low High Medium Maintain complete, documented setup instructions (README) so the system can be redeployed on another machine; keep all dependencies and versions recorded.
R5 Unauthorized access through shared or weak user credentials Security Low High Medium Enforce role-based access control; instruct users not to share credentials; the administrator assigns strong initial passwords; deactivate accounts of staff who leave the department.
R6 Administrator account compromise, granting full control of the system Security Low High Medium Limit the number of administrator accounts; use a strong administrator password; do not share the administrator account; all passwords are stored hashed using bcrypt.
R7 User resistance to adopting the new system over familiar manual methods Operational Medium Medium Medium Conduct a hands-on training and orientation session; provide an intuitive, simple interface; demonstrate the time saved compared to manual spreadsheet and paper methods.
R8 Users forgetting passwords; the system has no self-service password reset Operational Medium Low Low Administrators can manually reset user passwords; users can change their own password once logged in; self-service password reset is identified as a future enhancement.
R9 Incorrect or inconsistent data entry by users (e.g., wrong event dates or details) Operational Medium Medium Medium Apply input validation on both the frontend and backend; allow authorized users to edit records; require confirmation before destructive actions.
R10 Accidental deletion of events and their related tasks and documents Data Low Medium Low Require confirmation dialogs before deletion; restrict event deletion to administrators only; completed events are locked from editing to protect historical records.
R11 Concurrent editing conflicts when two users modify the same record simultaneously Technical Low Medium Low The small department size (approximately ten staff) makes simultaneous edits unlikely; record-level locking is identified as a future enhancement.
R12 Scope changes arising from panel or client feedback during development Project Medium Medium Medium Finalize and document requirements early; develop features in defined phases; record all approved changes and their rationale for traceability.
Risk Level Legend:

High Requires immediate, priority mitigation.

Medium Should be monitored and mitigated during development.

Low Acceptable; managed with standard practices.

Risk Level matrix: Likelihood (Low / Medium / High) × Impact (Low / Medium / High). High likelihood with High impact yields High risk; Low likelihood with Low impact yields Low risk, and so on.
